01 · About

Not just a videographer — a creative partner for your story.

Every frame is shot with intent. Every cut is paced like a film, not a feed post.

I'm Rabeeh Illikkal, a mobile-first videographer and editor working across Kerala. What started as a passion for capturing weddings on a phone has grown into a full creative practice — cinematic storytelling without the weight of a full camera crew.

My approach blends documentary honesty with a director's sense of pacing: natural light, deliberate movement, sound design and color grading that feels premium, personal, and unmistakably yours.

Every client gets a process built around trust — clear timelines, honest pricing, and a final film that actually feels like the day it was made on.
